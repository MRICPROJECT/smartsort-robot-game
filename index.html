Robot’s reminder
Smart sorting starts with us. Sensors can help, but they can make mistakes—and good waste management also needs the right collection and processing systems.

SmartSort is a developing student prototype. The current model uses moisture and suitable-metal detection with programmed rules; it does not use a camera or AI to identify every material.

Meet the real SmartSort 🤖

Our student prototype uses an IR sensor to detect an item, a moisture sensor as a clue for wet waste, an inductive sensor to detect suitable metals, and a controller to move a diverter towards a bin.

🍌WetFood scraps & peels
🧴DryPaper & suitable plastic
🥫OtherSuitable metals / assigned items

Important limitation: moisture is not the same as “food”, and the metal sensor does not detect every kind of waste. The current demonstration is intended for one item at a time, not a mixed handful. Public-space use would require further design, testing and safety checks.
